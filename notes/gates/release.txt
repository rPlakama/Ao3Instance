- `services.ao3instance.writesEnabled = true` is the declarative equivalent of
  AO3I_WRITE_ENABLED=true; writes still need a usable AO3 session, imported on
  /session (both cookies). With writes on and no usable session the UI says the
  session is unusable instead of misreporting sign-in.
- No application behaviour changed in this patch release; the write surface is
  the v0.1.5 feature set.
